Before you hit the track

Ready to
compete?

A short setup checklist to keep competition fair, footage clear, and race day running smoothly.

↻

Install the demo

Have the most up-to-date version of the Cranked demo installed. The Patreon version gives access to more maps, but CPL will use the demo for now to keep the league inclusive.

▣

Record your run

You will need recording software that can capture at least 720p resolution, including in-game audio.

◉

Be ready to stream

You must be able to cast your live Cranked gameplay to a Discord call when you are called up on race day.

Phase 02 / Race day

Show up.
Send it.

Once qualifying is complete and the athletes are ready, everyone joins the Cranked Pro League stage. Athletes are called up from slowest to fastest qualifying time to stream their gameplay.

Live event protocolOne stage · Three laps
01 / GET CALLED UP

Line up

Athletes are called in qualifying-time order, from slowest to fastest. Be ready when your turn comes around.

02 / SET YOUR BEST

Three laps

Each athlete gets three laps to set their best time. Make them count.

03 / THE STANDINGS

Points awarded

At the end of the stream, athletes receive their points and the standings are updated.
